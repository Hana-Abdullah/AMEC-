AMEC offers an affordable,
integrated assistive solution for
individuals with quadruple or tripodal
amputations, combining an electric
wheelchair and robotic arms with 6
degrees of freedom into one
system, eliminating the need for
multiple separate assistive devices.
Unlike conventional prosthetic
solutions, AMEC is designed to be
customizable to users’ individual
needs, abilities, and preferences,
providing a more accessible and
adaptable way to perform daily
activities at a significantly lower
cost.
Beyond the technology, AMEC
extends its impact through a
supportive community where users
can connect and share experiences,
alongside caregiver assistance
services and training programs that
empower families and caregivers to
provide effective support.

Cost Structure

Revenue Stream

Fixed Costs:

Direct Product Sales: Selling AMEC directly to customers or through healthcare providers and rehabilitation
centers.
Insurance Reimbursements: Establishing partnerships with insurance providers to facilitate coverage or
reimbursement for eligible users.
Leasing or Rental Options: Offering flexible leasing and rental plans for individuals who cannot afford the full
upfront cost.
Service Fees: Generating revenue through maintenance, repairs, technical support, adjustments, and periodic
servicing.
Caregiver Assistance Services: Providing trained caregivers or personal assistants to support users in operating
AMEC and performing daily activities when additional assistance is needed.
Caregiver Training Programs: Offering structured, paid training programs for family members, caregivers, or
personal assistants to develop the skills needed to safely assist AMEC users and operate its assistive features.
Government Grants and Funding: Applying for government grants, innovation funds, and disability-support funding
to support development, production, and accessibility initiatives.
